About these numbers

Spend is the sum of invoice distribution amounts (Inv Dist Amount) from Incorta's BSGiftsDonations business view. Invoice Amount isn't added up, because it repeats on every distribution line of an invoice. Some lines are reversals, so a vendor or year can net to less than its gross bookings. All rows are country AMERICA; the view's HCP classification, reportable supplier and department fields are empty, so they aren't shown.
